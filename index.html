About the group

We read important current work in AI and discuss its conceptual, methodological, and philosophical implications. Our conversations bring together perspectives from philosophy, logic, computer science, machine learning, and related areas.

For Fall 2026, our focus is interpretability and AI safety. The group is independently organized, with participants from different institutions.
